About Sri Sambodhi Viharaya – Buddhist Temple in Ragama, Sri Lanka

Sri Sambodhi Viharaya is a historic Buddhist temple located in Ragama, Sri Lanka, set beside a peaceful open field and centered around a sacred, centuries-old Bodhi tree. The temple serves the local community through daily Buddhist practice, weekly Bodhi Pooja ceremonies, and monthly Poya day sil programs guided by the resident Maha Sangha.

We welcome donations and charity contributions from devotees and well-wishers both in Sri Lanka and abroad to support our ongoing stupa construction project, temple maintenance, and community dana (almsgiving) programs. Every contribution, large or small, helps sustain this sacred site for future generations.

Whether you are looking for a Buddhist temple near Ragama, wish to make a donation to a Sri Lankan temple, or want to support ongoing charity and religious programs, Sri Sambodhi Viharaya welcomes your visit and your support.
